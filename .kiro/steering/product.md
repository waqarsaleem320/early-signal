# Product

PoultryRescue helps a small or very small ground poultry plant decide what to do with a lot.
The user is the plant's QA or HACCP lead. A qualified person reviews every high-consequence decision.

- Never name a plant in any output.
- Never claim to predict outbreaks or count illnesses avoided.
- Positive raw poultry is generally not adulterated. The exception is the 2024 determination for raw
  breaded stuffed chicken at 1 CFU/g or more.
- State every validation result, including the ones that failed.
